# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors to Yuzhuo Jia's personal website, including research collaborators and people exploring his photography, on desktop and mobile browsers. They should be able to identify the researcher, browse publications and photographs, and find contact details and the CV.

## Product Purpose

A personal research and photography portfolio. Preserve the existing factual content and make it comfortable to read and operate across screen sizes and input methods.

## Brand Personality

Dark, restrained, technical, with a photographic darkroom identity. The established visual language combines cyan details, film grain, frame markings, serif accents, decoding text, a targeting cursor, and film-roll interactions.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic portfolio template, excessive neon, or replacing the photographic identity with a conventional gallery grid. Preserve the current animation intensity, as explicitly requested on 2026-10-21.

## Design Principles

- Keep existing content and the established visual identity.
- Preserve current effects while making their controls and feedback clear.
- Give mobile layouts their own composition without hiding important information.
- Match visible interaction states to usable pointer, touch, and keyboard behavior.
- Make important secondary information readable; reserve microtype for decorative details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep the existing reduced-motion and coarse-pointer adaptations. Support keyboard focus, proper modal isolation, readable type, responsive text, and comfortable touch targets.

## Context

Based on the previously confirmed design in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-07-personal-site-redesign-design.md` and the user's 2026-10-21 request to implement the first review round: lightbox, mobile layout, small text, and hit areas. This round does not redesign the desktop composition, add content, or deploy the site.
